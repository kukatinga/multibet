SportPesa Jackpot: how it works and how we analyse a card

The Mega Jackpot rewards the right seventeen calls — but it's the bonus line most players actually reach. Here's how the games are chosen, what the numbers mean, and the factors we weigh every week.

John Sangini MULTIBET Editorial · 18 Oct 2026
6 min read 18+ only

1What the SportPesa Jackpot actually is

The SportPesa Mega Jackpot asks you to predict the outcome of seventeen football matches for a stake of KSh 99. Each game has three possible results — a home win, a draw or an away win — and you get one pick per game. Get all seventeen right, and you win the full jackpot.

That headline figure is the reason people play. But the jackpot is only the top prize. The bonus line — twelve correct out of seventeen — pays out far more often, and for most players it is the realistic target.

3Why the card is hard to call

Jackpot fixtures are not the obvious ones. They are chosen to be unpredictable — mid-table clashes, form teams against struggling names, and derbies where the league table tells you very little. A quick glance at the standings is rarely enough.

That is also why the same seventeen games often produce a wide spread of results, and why the bonus line — not the jackpot — is what most tickets realistically chase.

4What we look at when we analyse a card

No single stat picks a jackpot. Our approach is to combine several angles for every game, and then flag each one as either a stronger pick or an upset risk. The factors we lean on most:

Team form

Recent form is the most reliable starting point, provided you look past a single result. A side that has won four of its last six is genuinely in better shape than one that has won one — even if the league table suggests otherwise. Squads that have played together for a while tend to find consistency that the sum of their parts would not predict.

The Leicester City 2015–16 season is the classic example — a squad that had played together in the lower leagues before promotion, then won the Premier League on chemistry more than star power.

Home and away performance

Home advantage in the jackpot is not a myth. Teams that are strong at home — and struggle away from home — are worth far more attention than their overall points suggest. The reverse is just as useful: some sides travel badly regardless of where they sit in the table.

Head-to-head history

Some fixtures are stuck in a pattern. A side that has not beaten a rival in years sometimes keeps that record going regardless of current form. When a head-to-head record clashes with the league table, it is worth checking whether the pattern is real (a tactical mismatch) or just coincidence (a small sample).

Injuries and suspensions

Losing an anchor player — a striker who carries a team's goals, or the defensive midfielder who holds the shape — is one of the most under-priced shifts in football. Team news can swing a pick from a strong banker to an upset risk in a single afternoon. Most clubs publish injury updates through their own channels, and dedicated trackers such as Physioroom keep a current list for the English Premier League.

Motivation

Late in a season, fixtures take on extra weight. Relegation battles, European qualification, cup ties, and derbies change how teams play. A side with nothing to play for is not the same side as one fighting for its season. The relegation run-ins of recent Premier League seasons are a good reminder of how much extra performance a team can find when survival is on the line.

5Bonuses vs the full jackpot

The full jackpot is the headline. The bonus line is the goal we mostly aim for. In practice, most weeks that means building a card that optimises for twelve correct rather than seventeen — which is a meaningfully different exercise.

Where those two goals diverge, we say so on the prediction page. Where they align — a card with genuinely strong bankers across most of it — we say that too. Every week, every result, wins and misses, is published on our results page.

There is no shortcut to seventeen correct. There is a defensible method for reaching twelve.

6What winning actually changes

A full Mega Jackpot win is life-changing money. A bonus payout is far more modest but meaningful. Either way, the sensible move after a win is to speak to a licensed financial advisor before making any big decisions. Outlets like Standard Media have covered the advice past jackpot winners received — Treasury bills and bonds, not impulse purchases.

We treat betting as entertainment with a cost. Never stake money you cannot afford to lose. If it stops being entertainment, stop.
