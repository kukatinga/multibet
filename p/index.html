Manchester United vs Arsenal: form, head-to-head, team news and prediction

A full breakdown of Sunday's Premier League clash at Old Trafford — where the game will be won, who's missing, what the stats say, and where the value sits.

CompetitionPremier League
VenueOld Trafford
KickoffSun 1 Nov · 17:30
MatchdayRound 11

Manchester United host Arsenal at Old Trafford on Sunday evening with both sides in very different places. United are unbeaten in four but have drawn three of them, while Arsenal sit two points clear at the top of the Premier League and have won five of their last six. The fixture has produced goals, red cards and late drama in recent seasons — and this edition has plenty riding on it.

Below we break down the form, the head-to-head record, the team news, the tactical battle and — most importantly — where the betting value sits. Everything you need in one place.

Recent form

United have been steady rather than spectacular. Ruben Amorim's side have conceded just three goals in their last five Premier League matches but have struggled to convert territorial control into wins — a problem that cost them two points against Brentford and another two against Brighton.

Arsenal, by contrast, have clicked. Mikel Arteta's men have scored 14 goals in their last six matches across all competitions, with Bukayo Saka and Kai Havertz combining for nine of them.

Head-to-head record

Old Trafford used to be a fortress against Arsenal — United won 11 of 14 home league meetings between 2005 and 2019. That edge has flipped. Arsenal have won three of the last five at Old Trafford in all competitions, and the last two Premier League meetings in Manchester have both ended in Arsenal victories.

Reading the table

United's defensive numbers are genuinely good — only the top three sides have conceded fewer goals. Arsenal's edge is in chance creation: they generate nearly 40% more shots on target per game and are the league's best set-piece side. If United are to get anything from this, it will be through defending well and hitting on the counter.

Tactical breakdown

Both managers favour a back three in possession, which creates an unusual dynamic: neither side will have a natural spare man in midfield. The game will likely be decided in two specific zones.

1. United's left vs Arsenal's right

Saka will spend most of the evening attacking United's left. If Shaw doesn't start, United's left-back will face a winger who has beaten his man 62% of the time this season. That's the single biggest mismatch on the pitch. United will likely double up — expect a winger dropping in or a midfielder covering — which will pull Bruno Fernandes away from the centre and blunt United's counter-attacking threat.

2. Arsenal's set pieces vs United's aerial defence

Arsenal have scored six set-piece goals this season, the most in the league. United have conceded from corners twice — both in matches where Martínez was absent. If Martínez starts, United's aerial defence improves significantly; if he doesn't, expect Arsenal to test Onana early from dead balls.

3. The tempo battle

United want a slower game with long spells of defending and short bursts forward. Arsenal want to dominate the ball and force United to chase. Whichever side controls the tempo for the first 20 minutes will likely control the result.

Prediction

Arsenal are the better side right now — better form, better chance creation, better away record. But Old Trafford remains a difficult ground, and United's defensive numbers are genuinely strong. Historically, these games are tight, low-scoring and decided by fine margins.

Our read: Arsenal to win narrowly, with under 2.5 goals. The most likely correct score is 1-0 to Arsenal, followed by 1-1.

Frequently asked questions

When is Manchester United vs Arsenal?
