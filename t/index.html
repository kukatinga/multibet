How we approach jackpot games

Jackpot fixtures are chosen to be hard to call, so a simple look at the league table is not enough. For each game we look at:

  • Recent form over the last several matches
  • Head-to-head history, where it is relevant
  • Home and away performance
  • Injuries and suspensions
  • Motivation, such as cup ties, derbies and relegation battles

We then mark each game as a banker (a stronger pick) or an upset risk, and we publish the outcome of both after every draw. No method wins every week, and we don't claim one does.

Questions about the predictions

How do I receive the predictions?

After you approve the M-Pesa prompt, the predictions are sent by SMS to the number you paid with.

I paid but didn't get the SMS. What now?

Wait a few minutes first, since networks can be slow. If it still hasn't arrived, use our contact page and include the phone number you paid with and your M-Pesa confirmation code.

Does one payment cover other jackpots?

Each payment is for one set of predictions. Midweek and Betika jackpots are covered on their own page.

Are the predictions guaranteed?

No. They are analysis, and no one can promise a jackpot win. That is why we publish every result. You must be 18 or older to bet.
